Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

ZeroWallet ("we", "our", or "the app") is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use ZeroWallet.

1. We Do NOT Collect Your Private Keys or Mnemonics

ZeroWallet is a self-custody wallet. Your private keys, mnemonics (seed phrases), and passwords are generated and stored exclusively on your device. They are encrypted (XOR + SHA-256 with a random initialization vector) before being stored locally. We do not have access to them, we cannot recover them for you, and they never leave your device.

2. No Account Registration

ZeroWallet does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personal information. You can use all core wallet features without identifying yourself.

3. What Information Is Stored Locally

The following data is stored solely on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Encrypted private keys and mnemonics
  • Wallet names and preferences you set
  • Transaction history (fetched from public blockchains and cached locally)
  • App settings (language, theme, developer mode)

4. Data You Broadcast to Public Blockchains

When you send a transaction, the transaction data — including your wallet address, the recipient's address, and the amount — is broadcast to the relevant blockchain network. Blockchain transactions are public and immutable. This is inherent to how blockchains operate and is not controlled by ZeroWallet.

5. Third-Party Services

ZeroWallet interacts with the following third-party services to provide certain features. These services may receive your wallet address or transaction details as necessary to fulfill your requests:

  • Blockchain RPC Nodes: Public or user-configured RPC endpoints are used to query blockchain data and broadcast transactions.
  • Decentralized Exchange (DEX) Aggregators: OKX DEX and Jupiter aggregator APIs receive swap requests. They may receive your wallet address and the transaction parameters required for the swap.
  • Price Feeds: Price data is fetched from Binance, Bitget, DexScreener, and API Ninjas via a gateway service.
  • WalletConnect: When you connect to decentralized applications (dApps) via WalletConnect, a connection is established between your device and the dApp. ZeroWallet does not monitor or log these connections.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

ZeroWallet does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar). We do not track your in-app behavior, screen views, or feature usage.

7. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data on your device:

  • Mnemonic encryption using XOR with SHA-256 hashed password and random IV
  • Secure memory handling — sensitive key material is held in secure memory regions and zeroed after use
  • Password or biometric confirmation required for sensitive operations (exporting private keys, signing transactions)

8. Children's Privacy

ZeroWallet is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
