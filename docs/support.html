Frequently Asked Questions

Is ZeroWallet a custodial wallet?

No. ZeroWallet is a self-custody wallet. Your private keys and mnemonics are stored only on your device and are never shared with or accessible by us. We cannot recover your wallet for you.

I lost my mnemonic / seed phrase. Can you recover it?

No. As a self-custody wallet, we do not have access to your mnemonic. Please make sure to back it up securely and keep it in a safe place.

How do I import an existing wallet?

Open ZeroWallet, tap "Import Wallet", select "Mnemonic" as the import method, enter your 12 or 24-word mnemonic phrase, and give your wallet a name.

Which blockchains does ZeroWallet support?

ZeroWallet supports major blockchains including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Sui (SUI), Aptos (APT), TRON (TRX), and more.

How secure is ZeroWallet?

Your mnemonic is encrypted with XOR + SHA-256 using a random initialization vector before storage. Sensitive key material is managed in secure memory and zeroed after use. Sensitive operations require password confirmation.
